# Marketplace Dispute Policy (Mock)

## P1 — Amenity failure
If a listed amenity was not working and the problem was reported during the booking:
- One amenity failed: partial refund of 30%.
- Two or more amenities failed: partial refund of 50%.
A failure must be supported by evidence (ticket, photo, log) or admitted by the owner.

## P2 — Access failure
If the customer was denied access to a reserved space (locked, double-booked, owner unavailable) and no equivalent space was accepted: full refund of 100%.

## P3 — Cancellation by customer
Refund depends on how long before the booking start time the customer cancelled:
- More than 24 hours before: 100%.
- Between 2 and 24 hours before: 50%.
- Less than 2 hours before: 0%.
No exceptions apply.

## P4 — Property damage claims
A damage claim by the owner requires photos or timestamped evidence. Without such evidence, no damage deduction may be decided automatically and the case goes to manual review.

## P5 — Noise complaints
A refund for noise is allowed only if the listing explicitly promised a quiet environment AND there is evidence of the noise. If the listing does not promise a quiet environment, no refund is due under this clause, regardless of evidence. If the listing promised quiet but there is no evidence, the case goes to manual review.

## P6 — Insufficient evidence rule
If a fact that decides the outcome is disputed and no evidence is available to settle it, the recommendation must be manual review. This applies only when the outcome depends on that disputed fact.
